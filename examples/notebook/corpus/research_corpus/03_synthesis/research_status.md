# Research Status

## Current Status
- Phase 1–9 completed. 
- Phase 10 (Construction of Action Initiation Corpus) pending.

## Epistemological Boundaries & Evidence
- Current evidence supports a distinction between behavioral occurrence and subjective experiences such as desire, self-image, and explicit intention within the individual's records.
- However, the corpus does not yet establish a causal mechanism explaining this distinction.
- The distinction between known/repeated action forms and genuinely novel action forms remains under audit.
- Missing descriptions of action initiation are treated as **missing data**, not evidence of unconscious or automatic processing.

## Crucial Restrictions
- **No generalization to human behavior is permitted.** The findings represent patterns within this specific phenomenological corpus only.
- **Phase 5 classification is provisional.** Novel content must not be equated with novel action. The four-quadrant map of action initiation is a provisional research tool, not a finalized behavioral classification.
