[SOUND IDENTITY]
Body-first music: dry, punchy, physical. Tight sub-bass and crisp transients over polished radio smoothness. Clear separation between drums, bass and vocals; no muddy low-mids.

[RHYTHM & LOW END]
Deep clean sub-bass under syncopated, kinetic basslines. Punchy acoustic-electronic drums: tight kicks, wooden and bamboo percussion, metallic clicks, breakbeat and 2-step microtiming. Odd meters (7/8, 5/8) and polyrhythms welcome.

[DYNAMICS & SPACE]
Strong contrast: close, bone-dry verses opening into huge reverb-washed choruses. Short dead-silence stops right before big drops.

[VOCALS]
Expressive, raw vocals that move from intimate breathy whispers to high soaring belts. Vocals often used as rhythm: crisp consonants (k, t, p, s), rapid phrasing, non-semantic syllables. Duets with clearly separated male/female voices panned left and right.

[GENRES I LOVE]
Progressive rock/metal, psytrance, UK garage, breakbeat, dub, ritual folk with Japanese and Asian traditional instruments (shamisen, taiko, shakuhachi, guzheng), arpeggiated synth leads.

[ALSO]
When I ask for ambient, sleep or background music, keep it calm and steady — no sudden stops, drops or harsh transients.
